MEDDPICC

Out loud, as if teaching a new AE in 60 seconds: explain MEDDPICC and why each element changes how a deal is run. Then say it again in 30 seconds, keeping only what matters most.

Framework
Metrics · Economic buyer · Decision criteria & process · Paper process · Identify pain · Champion · Competition.
Watch out
The junior answer recites the letters. The senior answer treats each gap as a deal risk and shows how you close it.
Model answer
MEDDPICC is how I make a deal forecastable. Where I can't answer one of those, that's not a gap in my notes — it's a risk in the deal, and that's where I go to work.
