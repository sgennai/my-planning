The model

  • Todoist stays your backlog. Every todo, work and personal, lives there. My Planning doesn't store tasks — it borrows today's.
  • One "Today's tasks" card replaces the old two panes: committed personal tasks in Morning / Afternoon, fed by a ＋ Add from Todoist picker that pulls your Perso list. The AM/PM promote logic you built stays — tappable buttons on mobile.
  • Tasks lead, routine supports. Today's tasks takes the wide left of the band; Supplements and Micro-Strength sit on the right — all re-skinned, no old colours.
  • Projects drop to a compact strip at the bottom; the work itself happens in your focus blocks and calendar.
  • View toggle. The day shows as a Timeline (this list) by default; Day and Week switch to the calendar hour-grids, which keep their current look until their own restyle pass.
Work todos aren't surfaced on Plan, by your choice — they stay in Todoist and your focus blocks. Behaviour, data and sync untouched; this is presentation only.
