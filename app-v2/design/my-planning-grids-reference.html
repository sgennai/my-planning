My Planning · Day & Week grids

The hour-grid, finally drawn.

The first two references deliberately left the calendar surface open. This is it — the Day and Week grids in the same language: ink on paper, one warm signal, events that read at a glance. It's designed to sit at full width as its own view and to read well narrower, inside the Plan screen's left column. The app already handles drag-to-schedule, resize, and overlap packing; this is how that surface should look, not how it behaves.

Grid decisions

  • Calm density. One hour ≈ 46px in Day, ≈ 38px in the tighter Week. Hairline hour lines, mono labels in the gutter — structure you can ignore until you need it.
  • Events read title-first. Title in ink, time in mono underneath; category as a 3px left edge plus a faint matching fill. Past events fade to ~50% so the eye lands on what's ahead.
  • Overlaps split side-by-side within the day; the now-line is a 2px rule with a dot, on the current day only.
  • All-day is a sticky strip of chips under the headers — never full-height blocks competing with timed events.
  • Week is Mon–Sun, today's header in gold, weekend columns dimmed with a collapse control, and the Routine · Work · Household layer toggles live up top.
